A simple checklist for discussing electrical work

The exact work depends on the conditions at your property. An electrician may need to examine the relevant part of the electrical system before describing possible work. For a repair, point out the affected area and explain what has happened. For an installation or alteration, describe the intended use and location.

Have these details ready before you call:
- The Salt Lake City address where the work is needed.
- A plain-language summary of the issue or project.
- Any pattern you’ve noticed, such as when the problem appears.
- Your preferred way to coordinate access to the property.

Don’t make a repair just to test whether the problem goes away. Avoid covering up a symptom or changing equipment before an electrician can understand the request. If you’re worried about immediate safety, say that directly when you call and follow appropriate safety guidance rather than handling exposed or damaged equipment.
