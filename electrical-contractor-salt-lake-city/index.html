A practical project checklist

Gather what you can before you call. It’s fine if you don’t have every answer yet, but these points can help organize your request:
- The property address and type of property.
- A plain-language description of the electrical work you want.
- The rooms or areas involved.
- Any timing considerations or related work already planned.
- Questions about how the scope will be discussed.

Avoid starting electrical work based on assumptions about what an existing system can support. Don’t remove covers or attempt wiring changes to prepare for a visit. If the request is tied to a larger renovation or another trade’s schedule, say so early. That context can help clarify what needs to be discussed first.
